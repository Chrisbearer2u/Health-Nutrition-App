## What the App does NOT do

- The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name.
- The App does not use advertising or analytics SDKs.
- The App does not access your contacts, location, photos, files, health
  data, or any other data on your device. Its only permission is internet
  access.
- The App does not sell or rent any information. There is nothing to sell.

## Information processed

### Local information

- **Browsing and search activity** stays entirely on your device.
  The organ/disease/food database is bundled with the App and works offline.
- **Chat conversations** are held in the app's memory while it is open.
  Closing the chat clears it; the App does not store conversation history
  on your device between sessions.

### Information transmitted

- **Chat messages you send.** When you use the AI assistant, the text you
  type — and nothing else — is transmitted over an encrypted connection to
  our server, which forwards it to a third-party AI language model provider
  to generate a response. The response is streamed back to you and is not
  retained by us after the request completes.
- **Your IP address** is processed transiently by our server solely to
  prevent abuse (rate limiting), and is not stored.
- We do not transmit any device identifiers, and the App contains no
  tracking mechanisms of any kind.
