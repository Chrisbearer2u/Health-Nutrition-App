## NutriGuide — Medical Disclaimer

**NutriGuide is an informational and educational resource only. It does not
provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.**

The content in this app — including disease descriptions, causes, effects,
and any foods or food supplements mentioned — is provided for general
wellness and educational purposes. It is drawn from public health sources
(such as the World Health Organization and the U.S. National Institutes of
Health) and peer-reviewed nutrition research, and is summarized in plain
language. It has not been evaluated for your individual health situation.

**Always seek the advice of a qualified healthcare professional** with any
questions you may have regarding a medical condition or before making any
changes to your diet, starting or stopping any supplement, or beginning any
health, nutrition, or exercise program. **Never disregard professional
medical advice or delay in seeking it because of something you have read in
this app.**

- Nothing in this app is intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any
  disease.
- Statements about foods and supplements describe general research findings
  and traditional use; they are not prescriptions, dosing instructions, or
  guarantees of results.
- Food supplements can interact with medications, be harmful in certain
  conditions, or be unsafe during pregnancy or for children. Consult a
  pharmacist or physician before taking any supplement.
- **If you think you may have a medical emergency, call your doctor or
  emergency services immediately.**
- AI-generated chat responses are produced by a language model, may contain
  errors, and must not be relied upon as a substitute for professional
  medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If anything in this app conflicts with guidance from your healthcare
provider, follow your provider's guidance.
